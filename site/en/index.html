Trax

Scent tracking training for dogs

The track layer walks the route and marks articles along the way. When the dog runs the track, Trax shows the dog's path on top of the laid track, alerts you as you approach an article, and records results, track age and weather for every session.

One-time purchase · No account · No in-app purchases

View Trax on the App Store

HockeySub

Line changes and ice time in youth hockey

HockeySub helps the coach give every player equal ice time. The app tells you when it is time to change and who goes on and off, even on the lock screen, and keeps track of playing time, results and season stats.
