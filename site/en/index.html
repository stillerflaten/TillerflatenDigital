Trax

Tracking training for dogs

The track layer walks the route and marks articles along the way. When the dog runs the track, Trax shows the dog's path on top of the laid track, alerts you as you approach an article, and records results, track age and weather for every session.

One-time purchase · No account · No in-app purchases

Learn more at trax-dogtracking.no →

HockeySub

Line changes and ice time in youth hockey

HockeySub helps the coach give every player equal ice time. The app tells you when it is time to change and who goes on and off, even on the lock screen, and keeps track of playing time, results and season stats.

Learn more at hockeysub.no →

Aktivitetsdag

Planning activity days in youth sports

Aktivitetsdag helps coaches plan activity days in ice hockey, football, handball and other sports. It splits players into teams so children get new teammates, builds a match schedule where every team plays the same number of games, and shares out referees and parent tasks fairly. Everything can be printed or saved as PDF. The site is in Norwegian.

Website · No login · Everything stays in your browser

Visit aktivitetsdag.org →

About

I'm Silje Tillerflaten, and I run Tillerflaten Digital from Trondheim, Norway. I build apps for iPhone and iPad, and the websites that go with them.

I have learned the craft by building things people actually use. HockeySub helps coaches share ice time fairly in youth hockey, Aktivitetsdag makes planning sports activity days easier, and Trax is now on the App Store and Google Play for anyone who does tracking training with their dog.

When something gets stuck, I usually find a way around it. What I do best is making things clear and easy to use, and making them look good.

Tillerflaten Digital is a sole proprietorship, so when you get in touch, you talk to me. I build software that respects its users: clear choices, no unnecessary data collection, and design that feels natural.
