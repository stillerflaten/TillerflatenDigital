Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data I process when you visit tillerflatendigital.no or get in touch, why, and what rights you have.

In short

The website uses no cookies or advertising. I count visits anonymously with GoatCounter, which neither stores your IP address nor tracks you across websites. Beyond that, I only receive information about you if you send me a message yourself.

Who is responsible

Tillerflaten Digital, a sole proprietorship run by Silje Tillerflaten (org. no. 938 657 742), is the data controller. You can reach me at post@tillerflatendigital.no.

When you send the contact form

What
Your name, email address, what your enquiry is about and what you write in your message.
Why
To reply to your enquiry. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in being able to reply to messages I receive (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
How
The form is sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards it to my email. FormSubmit may process the data outside the EEA. Before the form is sent, FormSubmit may show a spam check from Google (reCAPTCHA), which may set cookies on FormSubmit's page.

If you would rather avoid this, you can email me directly at post@tillerflatendigital.no.

When you send an email

Email to me is stored with Domene.no (Domene AS), which has its servers in Norway.

When you visit the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub stores visitors' IP addresses in technical logs for security purposes, see GitHub's privacy statement. I do not have access to these logs.

To see how many people visit the site, I use the visitor counter GoatCounter (goatcounter.com). It records which page was viewed, which page you came from, browser type, screen size and approximate country. GoatCounter uses no cookies and does not store your IP address, so the numbers cannot be linked to you as a person. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

How long data is kept

Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted no later than 12 months after our last contact. If we work together, I keep our correspondence for as long as the customer relationship lasts. Invoices and other accounting records are kept for five years, as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act.

Sharing

I do not sell your data or share it with anyone other than the services mentioned above.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. You can also ask me to restrict processing, object to it, or receive a copy of your data. Email post@tillerflatendigital.no and I will reply within 30 days. If you believe I am handling your data incorrectly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no).

Changes

I update this policy when needed. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
